Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Everything travelers usually ask before booking a tour with us. Can't find your answer? Reach out and we'll help directly.

01.

What kinds of trips do you offer?

From single-day city tours to multi-day Nile itineraries — day trips, private guided tours, and fully customized multi-city packages.

02.

How do I book a trip?

Choose a tour, pick your dates and group size, and confirm with a deposit online — the rest is handled by our local team.

03.

What documents do I need for my trip?

A passport valid for at least 6 months past your travel date, plus a visa — which most nationalities can get on arrival.

04.

Can I change my travel dates after booking?

Yes, free of charge up to 14 days before departure, subject to availability on the new dates.

05.

What's your refund policy?

Full refund up to 30 days before your tour, 50% up to 7 days before, and no refund inside 7 days of departure.

06.

Can I get a custom trip tailored to my preferences?

Absolutely — tell us your interests, pace, and budget, and our team will build a private itinerary around them.

07.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes, groups of 6 or more get tiered pricing — reach out with your group size and we'll send a custom quote.
